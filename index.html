02 — Work

What we’ve built.

Three systems in detail, with the tools behind them.

01  ·  Education platform

Geo-Books

One app for exam practice and university life, aimed at JAMB and WAEC candidates and university students in Nigeria.

  • Firebase
  • Node.js
  • Express
  • Cloudflare R2
  • Computer-based test practice on real past-question banks, with performance analytics and bookmarks.
  • An admission predictor, AI explanations for answers, flashcards and a world ranking leaderboard.
  • University tools: hostel finder, CGPA calculator, and lectures with a lecturer dashboard for uploading courses.
  • A tutor hub where independent tutors publish courses, and a marketplace with seller profiles and escrow.
02  ·  E-learning site

MedPhysio

A physiology course site built for a lecturer who wanted to teach and charge online without a developer on call.

  • Next.js
  • SQLite
  • OpenAI
  • Railway
  • Student accounts and login, with courses and lessons the lecturer uploads from an admin panel.
  • An AI assistant that answers student questions on the course material.
  • Subscriptions paid by bank transfer, with a one-tap WhatsApp notice to the admin for verification.
  • Admin pages for payments, lecturers and analytics, protected behind login.

05 — Questions

Before you reach out.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We ask in plain language what takes up your time, then build around it. You run the finished system from an admin panel that needs no coding.

Can customers order and pay through WhatsApp and bank transfer?

Yes. We have built WhatsApp-based ordering, and bank-transfer payments where an admin verifies each payment before access is granted.

How long does a build take, and what does it cost?

Both depend on the scope. After the brief we give you a fixed package with the price, so you know the cost before any work starts.

What happens after launch?

We stay close through launch to fix what real use turns up. After that you manage content, users and payments yourself from the admin panel.

Do you build for businesses outside Nigeria?

Yes. Ballast is built for a global market, with card billing through Stripe.
